A clearer way to organize research notes

Research notes become more useful when the important ideas stay close to the evidence that supports them. A reliable reading workflow should keep the original source available without making every navigation element part of the stored document.

The article's second paragraph explains why a reader-focused parser should preserve the author's argument while omitting promotional and interface material. See the reading guidance for a related explanation. This makes the resulting text more suitable for search and chunking.

Meaningful headings, paragraphs, lists, and tables can remain useful when they belong to the main article. Scripts, styles, and unrelated page chrome should not occupy the document's text representation.
